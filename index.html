Prototype calculation notes

Rhythm = 50 × log₂(W+2 ÷ E), capped to 0–100.

Stress = 100 × (E ÷ W+2) × max(1, W ÷ W+2), capped to 0–100.

Numbers are rounded to whole points; the meter follows the unrounded value. Archetype remarks use a temporary reference: W 28, W+2 55, E 6. These are draft study scores, not validated clinical health or stress ratings.
